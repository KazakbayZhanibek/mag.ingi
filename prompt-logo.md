# Промпт для генерации логотипа MAGAS ENGINEERING

## Промпт (копируй целиком):

```
Design a minimalist, professional logo for "MAGAS ENGINEERING" — a construction and engineering company based in Almaty, Kazakhstan.

STYLE:
- Ultra minimalist, flat design, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects
- Clean geometric lines, sharp edges, no rounded corners
- Corporate, strict, professional feel
- Must look good at any size: from 16x16px favicon to large billboard

COLORS ONLY:
- Dark background: #0f1923 (dark charcoal/navy)
- Accent: #f5a623 (warm amber/orange)
- White: #ffffff

ICON/SYMBOL REQUIREMENTS:
- Must represent CONSTRUCTION, BUILDING, or ENGINEERING
- Use geometric shapes: rectangles, triangles, straight lines
- Avoid: circles, curves, organic shapes, complex details
- Should work as a standalone icon without text
- The icon should be recognizable at 16x16 pixels

TEXT LAYOUT:
- "MAGAS" in large, bold, uppercase letters (font weight 800-900)
- "ENGINEERING" in small, light, uppercase letters underneath (font weight 400-500, with wide letter spacing 3-4px)
- Font: Inter, Montserrat, or similar modern sans-serif

COMPOSITION:
- Icon on the left, text on the right (horizontal version)
- Icon alone (square version for favicon/avatar)
- Dark version (white/orange on dark background)
- Light version (dark/orange on white background)

AVOID:
- Too many details (must be simple)
- Gradients, glows, shadows
- Rounded shapes
- Abstract unclear symbols
- Decorative elements
- Multiple colors (max 3)

DELIVERABLES:
- SVG vector file
- PNG with transparent background (512x512, 192x192, 32x32)

REFERENCE KEYWORDS:
minimalist construction logo, architecture firm logo, engineering company symbol, building icon geometric, corporate construction branding
```

## Альтернативный промпт (если нужен другой вариант):

```
Create a monogram logo for "MAGAS ENGINEERING" — a Kazakhstani construction company.

The letter "M" should be stylized as a building or architectural structure:
- Two vertical pillars forming the legs of M
- A triangular roof connecting them at the top
- Small rectangular windows inside the M shape
- A small antenna or crane element on top

Style: flat, geometric, no gradients
Colors: #0f1923 (dark) + #f5a623 (orange accent)
Background: dark #0f1923 with orange icon
Must be ultra-simple, recognizable at 16px favicon size
```
